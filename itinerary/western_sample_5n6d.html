Day 1 · Waikiki (Oahu)

Arrival, resort welcome, sunset sail

Land at Daniel K. Inouye International (HNL). A Sample Tour Agency host meets you curbside and transfers you to a Waikiki beachfront resort. Afternoon at leisure — pool, sand, or a short walk along Kalakaua Avenue.

  • Resort check-in — ocean-view or partial-ocean room, double occupancy, as confirmed.
  • Welcome amenity — light tropical snack and sparkling cider in-room.
  • Sunset sail — 90-minute catamaran off Waikiki; soft drinks included. Overnight Waikiki.
Day 2 · Oahu circle day

North Shore towns and windward lookouts

Private driver for a relaxed island day. Coffee stop in Haleiwa, photo time at Waimea Bay (swim only when conditions allow), pineapple stand, then windward lookouts before returning to Waikiki. Overnight Waikiki.

Day 3 · Fly to Maui

Inter-island hop, Kaʻanapali beach time

Morning transfer to HNL, short flight to Kahului (OGG), then private transfer to a Kaʻanapali resort. Afternoon free on the beach path — swim, stroll, or spa add-on. Overnight Kaʻanapali.

Days 4 to 6 · inclusions
Day 4 · Road to Hana

Waterfalls, black sand, picnic lunch

Early start with a guided Road to Hana day (small group or private, as booked). Expect rainforest tunnels, roadside falls, a black-sand beach stop, and a packed picnic. Return to Kaʻanapali by early evening. Overnight Kaʻanapali.

Day 5 · Kaʻanapali leisure

Free morning, sunset catamaran

Morning at leisure — beach, pool, or optional snorkel at Black Rock (paid extras listed below). Late-afternoon sunset catamaran with light pupus and soft drinks. Final night on Maui.

Day 6 · Departure

Breakfast and airport transfer

Resort breakfast, then a timed private drop to Kahului Airport (OGG) for your mainland or onward flight.
